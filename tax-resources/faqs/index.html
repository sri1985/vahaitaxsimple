Common Questions

What does a tax consultant do?

A tax consultant prepares and files tax returns, plans to reduce tax legally, maintains accounting records, and helps clients respond to CRA notices, audits and appeals. A good consultant also explains your options clearly so you can make informed financial decisions throughout the year.

What happens if I file my taxes late?

If you owe tax, the CRA charges a late-filing penalty of 5 percent of the balance owing plus 1 percent for each full month late, up to 12 months. Interest also accrues daily. Filing on time, even if you cannot pay, reduces penalties.

Can you file prior-year or overdue tax returns?

Yes. You can file returns for past years at any time, and doing so may unlock unclaimed refunds, benefits and credits. We prepare overdue returns, assess possible penalties and, where appropriate, request relief or guide you through the CRA voluntary disclosures program.

Do I need to file a return if I had no income?

Filing is still recommended even with no income. A return establishes RRSP room and is needed to receive benefits and credits such as the GST/HST credit and the Canada Child Benefit. Filing also keeps your CRA records current.

What is the tax difference between a sole proprietorship and a corporation?

A sole proprietor reports business income on a personal T1 return and pays tax at personal rates. A corporation files a separate T2 return and pays corporate tax, while owners pay personal tax on salary or dividends taken. Corporations can defer tax on retained profits.

Can you represent me before the CRA?

Yes. With your proper authorization we can speak with the CRA on your behalf about audits, reviews, assessments and payment arrangements. You remain in control, and we keep you informed of every communication and deadline throughout the process.

What is the small business deduction?

The small business deduction reduces the federal tax rate on the first 500,000 dollars of active business income earned by Canadian-controlled private corporations. Combined with Ontario’s lower small business rate, it significantly reduces corporate tax on income that qualifies.

Are payment arrangements available if I cannot pay my tax debt?

Yes. If you cannot pay in full, the CRA may agree to a payment arrangement. Call the CRA payment arrangements line at 1-888-863-8657 or request one through CRA My Account, and we can help you prepare and present a realistic proposal.

How do I know a call from the CRA is genuine?

The CRA never demands immediate payment by gift card, cryptocurrency or prepaid card, and does not threaten arrest. If you are unsure, hang up and call the CRA directly using a number published on canada.ca, then contact us if you need help.
